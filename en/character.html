What You Will Do

Create characters Design avatars, personalities, and distinct conversation styles for every character.
Build story and world Write lore, backstory, and opening dialogue so the character feels alive from the first chat.
Grow a fanbase Share your characters, invite first users to try them, and build a community around them.

Who We Are Looking For

  • You enjoy writing characters, shaping dialogue, or building fictional worlds — no technical background required.
  • You have used or are familiar with AI chat apps like Doki, Character.AI, and similar products.
  • You can complete at least 1-3 characters within the agreed timeline.
  • You can manage your own time — the work is flexible and follows your rhythm.

Program Notes

General Terms

Automated detection: Characters that do not meet quality standards may be detected automatically and become ineligible for bonuses.

Quality criteria: Before submitting, read the official Review Criteria carefully.

Message counting: Only user messages are counted. Character replies are not included.

Beta program: This beta runs for 6 months with the first 2 bonus milestones, from Apr 15-Oct 15, 2026.

Terms & Policies

Character originality: Characters found to copy another creator's work will be removed from the platform and will not be eligible for any payment.

Content review: Moderation policies can vary by platform, so some characters may require more review time.

Limited slots: Applications may close early if creator slots are filled.
