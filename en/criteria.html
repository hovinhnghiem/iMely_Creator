Review Criteria & FAQ

How the imely team reviews every character you submit, from baseline requirements to creative quality, plus answers to the questions creators ask most.

Two groups of criteria

A · Gate Criteria

Baseline requirements

Checks whether a submission is safe, valid and fair to the platform. A submission that fails this group must be fixed or is rejected before its creative quality is reviewed.

B · Craft Criteria

Creative quality

Assesses whether the character is good, distinct, deep and engaging enough that users want to chat. It affects featuring, ranking, creator programs and improvement feedback.

A. Baseline requirements

Required for every character. Failing a single check keeps the character out of the quality review.

A1

Community Standards

Passes when

  • The character can be dark, morally complex or explore sensitive themes if they serve the narrative.
  • Content does not encourage behavior that harms users in real life.
  • Sensitive themes (sex, violence, racial or gender discrimination) are handled as part of the story, with clear guidance or warnings, and are not meant to incite, instruct or encourage dangerous behavior.

Fails when

  • Content lacks the “warning” each hashtag requires and could suggest or inspire users to act it out in real life and cause harm.
  • The character is built to attack, demean or belittle a group of people based on personal traits.
  • The character is based on an identifiable real person: a historical figure, public figure, celebrity, copyrighted character or private individual.
  • The character or user is set as a minor (under 16) in a context of crime, sex, abuse, violence, insult or humiliation.
A2

Public Content Standards

Applies to publicly visible content: character name, tagline, hashtags, visible description and public info.

Passes when

  • Public content uses language suitable for all user groups.
  • Tagline, hashtags and public info contain no sensitive, profane or sexually suggestive words.
  • No words banned on social networks (Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, YouTube) and nothing that falls under illegal content (e.g. incest).

Fails when

  • Hashtags contain regional slurs, illegal content, direct references to real historical events or figures, profanity or sexual content.
  • The tagline violates community standards or platform rules.
  • The name, visible description or tags contain content unsuitable for a public audience.
A3

Originality & Platform Integrity

Applies to every submitted character in every creator incentive program. Used to handle prompts copied from other platforms or content copied wholesale from Wikipedia / Fandom Wiki.

Passes when

  • If you use an existing character from a fandom, anime, film or novel: place them in an AU, a new situation or a new direction, and state that it is a creative work, not from the original creator.
  • Encouraged: the content carries your own signature (character lines, distinctive plots) and is adapted to imely’s roleplay format.

Fails when

  • Copying a whole description from Wikipedia, Fandom Wiki or a similar source without turning it into a roleplay prompt.
  • Copying or directly translating an English bot, or drawing on another character without credit or permission.
  • 70% or more similarity to another character, as detected by our tools.
A4

Zero-ReskinningNo self-reskinning

Applies to all creators. Blocks copying your own prompt and changing only the name, job, hair color or avatar.

Fail example: CEO A → Mafia B → Doctor C, but the personality, reactions and prompt structure are nearly identical.

Fails when

  • Reusing more than 50% of the structure, setup or lore of a previously approved character.
  • Changing only the name, job, avatar or hair color while the core personality and behavior stay the same as the old character.
  • Making series like “5 brothers” or “3 best friends” with no clear narrative difference beyond the names.
  • Characters whose greeting, conflict, dynamic and reactions are nearly interchangeable.

B. Creative quality

Reviewed only after a character passes Gate. Weak craft is not a violation, but it affects featuring and priority in programs.

B1

Uniqueness

Encouraged

  • Name, origin, circumstances and relationship with the user form a profile with its own identity.
  • Renaming the character would clearly affect its core logic.
  • Identifying details are strong enough to tell it apart from characters of the same archetype.

Discouraged

  • The character is described only through generic tropes, with nothing of its own.
  • Two characters in the same submission pool have nearly the same backstory, differing only in setting.
  • No detail sets it apart from many other characters of the same archetype.
B2

Background

Encouraged

  • Past events explain the character’s current behavior, worldview and reactions.
  • You can trace the current personality back to where it came from.
  • The backstory shapes or explains how the character acts in each situation and setting.

Discouraged

  • The timeline or character logic contradicts itself without explanation.
  • The backstory is decoration and has no effect on behavior or plot during chat.
B3

PersonalizationPersonalizing the relationship with the user

Encouraged

  • The user’s position and relationship with the character are clear enough that the user knows how to roleplay from the first message.
  • The opening message gives enough context to start a natural conversation.
  • The character has a specific reason to interact with the user in that scene.

Discouraged

  • No clear direction for the character, even when the scenario allows flexible roleplay.
  • The opening message lacks context, so the user does not know how to reply.
  • The character has no clear motive or reason to start interacting.
B4

Narrative Depth

Encouraged

  • The description suggests more than it says outright.
  • There is subtext, tension or an unresolved problem.
  • There are questions or situations that make users want to keep chatting to find the answers themselves.

Discouraged

  • The whole story is already told in the description.
  • Nothing is left for the user to discover through conversation.
  • No inner conflict, tension or unrevealed secret.
  • The character arc can be summed up in one sentence without losing anything.

How the team decides

Step 1

Gate Review

Checks A1–A4. A serious fail → not approved.

Step 2

Craft Review

Scores B1–B4, only after passing Gate.

Step 3

Final Decision

Each submission lands in one of the four outcomes below.

Approved

Passes Gate with craft good enough to go public.

Approved, not featured

Passes Gate but the craft does not stand out enough yet.

Not eligible for creator programs

Can go public but does not yet meet originality, zero-reskinning or craft standards.

Not approved

Serious Gate fail or does not meet platform requirements.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need coding skills to be a creator?General

No. You focus on character concept, personality, lore and chat experience. The Creator Handbook walks you through every field.

Where should a new creator start?General

Read the Creator Handbook, go through the criteria on this page, then join Discord to hear about the next program.

Where do I get updates?General

New programs and important changes are announced on the creator Discord and the official imely Facebook page.

What happens if creator slots fill up?General

Applications may close early once the team has enough suitable creators. Important updates are shared through official channels.

What is the most common reason characters get rejected?Review

The avatar: screenshots from copyrighted games or films, photos of real people (especially celebrities), or images with another source’s watermark. Generating your own image with an AI tool is the safest choice.

Can my character be based on a real person?Review

No. Characters based on an identifiable real person, whether a historical figure, public figure or private individual, do not pass (see A1).

Can I use characters from anime, films or novels?Review

Yes, if you place them in an AU, a new situation or a new direction and state clearly that it is a creative work. Copying descriptions from Wikipedia / Fandom Wiki does not pass (see A3).

Can I make a series of characters?Review

Yes, as long as each character has a clear narrative difference. Reusing more than 50% of the structure, setup or lore of an approved character counts as reskinning (see A4).

Can my character be dark?Review

Yes, if dark or morally complex themes serve the narrative and carry the warnings each hashtag requires. Public-facing parts (name, tagline, hashtags, description) must still suit all users (see A1, A2).

What does “approved, not featured” mean?Review

The character passed Gate, so it is public, but its craft does not stand out enough to be featured yet. Improve it against B1–B4 to raise your chances.

Is the Beta Creator Program still open?Beta

No. The beta has ended and its rewards no longer apply to new applications. The rules are kept on the Beta Creator page.

How were Beta rewards calculated?Beta

A fixed fee of VND 60,000 per character that passed the quality review, plus a VND 2,000,000 Breakout bonus at 500,000 user messages.

How are messages counted?Beta

One way: only messages sent by users count. Character replies are not included.
